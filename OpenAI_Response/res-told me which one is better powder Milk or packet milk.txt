There's no single "better" option between powdered milk and packet milk; it depends on your priorities and circumstances.  Here's a comparison:

**Powdered Milk:**

**Pros:**

* **Longer shelf life:**  Powdered milk can last for months or even years unopened, making it ideal for storage and emergencies.
* **Lightweight and compact:**  Easy to transport and store, requiring less space.
* **Cost-effective:** Often cheaper per serving than other milk options.

**Cons:**

* **Requires preparation:** Needs to be mixed with water, which can be inconvenient.
* **Different taste and texture:**  Can have a slightly different taste and texture than fresh milk; some find it less creamy.
* **Nutritional value:**  Some nutrients can be lost during the processing of powdered milk.


**Packet Milk (UHT Milk):**

**Pros:**

* **Convenience:** Ready to drink straight from the packet.
* **Taste and texture:**  Closer to the taste and texture of fresh milk.
* **Longer shelf life than fresh milk:** Though not as long as powdered milk, it still lasts for several weeks or months after opening.

**Cons:**

* **Shorter shelf life than powdered milk:**  Less suitable for long-term storage.
* **Higher cost:** Generally more expensive than powdered milk.
* **Packaging waste:** Creates more packaging waste than powdered milk.


**In short:**

* Choose **powdered milk** if you need a long shelf life, are on a budget, need something portable, or are prepping for emergencies.
* Choose **packet milk** if convenience and a taste closer to fresh milk are your top priorities.
